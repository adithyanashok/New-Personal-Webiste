GetADoctor

A healthcare website for Kerala that helps patients compare treatment options, understand costs, connect with specialists, and receive guided support through home care and surgery journeys.

Role Full-stack developer
Stack Next.js, responsive UI, SEO, enquiry forms
Healthcare Next.js Responsive UI SEO Lead Forms WhatsApp

What I built

  • Created a modern healthcare web experience focused on treatment discovery and patient enquiry conversion.
  • Built sections for surgery support, doctor-at-home services, nursing, lab tests, and specialty treatment categories.
  • Designed a clear patient journey from treatment enquiry to healthcare team follow-up and specialist coordination.
  • Added responsive layouts, contact forms, service navigation, and quick WhatsApp access for high-intent users.
  • Structured public-facing content around Kerala locations including Kochi, Kozhikode, and Trivandrum.

Why it matters

  • Shows product execution in a healthcare context where trust, clarity, and conversion all matter.
  • Turns complex surgical care decisions into a guided, approachable digital workflow.
  • Demonstrates SEO-aware page structure, strong content hierarchy, and mobile-first service discovery.
  • Connects online enquiry, clinical support, and real-world care coordination into one patient-facing platform.
